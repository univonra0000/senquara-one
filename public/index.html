📦 Step 5 — Inventory + Global Compliance Master

Global HS classification is based on the World Customs Organization Harmonized System. National tariff codes, taxes, exemptions and duties remain country-specific and must be checked against the relevant official authority. WCO says HS is used by more than 200 countries/economies and final classification is under the national tariff schedule.

🔎 HSN / HS Master

WCO HS is the international base; use the national tariff database for final classification.
India's GST Portal provides HSN/SAC search and an HSN directory download. CBIC publishes GST rates/classification and customs tariff resources.
